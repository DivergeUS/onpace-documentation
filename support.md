# OnPace Support

OnPace helps people create, organize, and participate in shared savings goals, group planning, invitations, budgets, and progress tracking.

OnPace is operated by Tyler King, doing business as OnPace.

## Contact Support

For help, feedback, bug reports, account questions, or safety concerns, contact:

**Email:** contactonpace@gmail.com

Please include:

- The email address associated with your OnPace account
- Your device model and iOS version
- Your OnPace app version
- A short description of the issue
- Screenshots or screen recordings, if safe to share

Do not send passwords, full payment-card information, government IDs, or other highly sensitive information by email.

## Sign-In Help

Confirm that you are using the same sign-in method and email address used to create your OnPace account.

If you cannot access your account, email contactonpace@gmail.com with a description of the problem.

## Group and Invitation Help

If you cannot join a group, ask the organizer to confirm that:

- The invitation was sent to the correct account or email address
- The invitation has not expired or been revoked
- The group is still active
- You are signed in to the intended OnPace account

## Group Privacy

Goal organizers may choose privacy settings that limit what group members can see.

Depending on the group’s selected settings, you may see:

- Exact savings amounts
- Percentage progress
- Contribution or pace status
- Aggregate group progress
- Less detailed progress information

Contact the group organizer if you have questions about group visibility.

## Purchases and Premium Access

If you purchased Group Pass or OnPace Plus but premium access is not active:

1. Confirm you are signed in to the intended OnPace account.
2. Open **Purchases & Premium** in the app.
3. Select **Restore Purchases**.
4. Confirm that the purchase was made with the intended Apple Account.
5. Close and reopen OnPace.

If premium access does not restore, email contactonpace@gmail.com and include:

- Your OnPace account email
- Approximate purchase date
- Product purchased
- A screenshot of the Apple receipt, if available

## Manage or Cancel an Apple Subscription

Apple manages subscription billing.

To manage or cancel an OnPace subscription:

1. Open the **Settings** app on your Apple device.
2. Tap your Apple Account name.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Select **OnPace**.
5. Select the available management or cancellation option.

Deleting your OnPace account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel the subscription through Apple Account settings to prevent future renewal charges.

## Report a User or Content

If you see abusive, threatening, fraudulent, illegal, or otherwise inappropriate behavior or content, contact contactonpace@gmail.com.

Please include:

- The relevant group or goal name
- The display name or account involved, if available
- A description of the issue
- Screenshots, if safe to provide

We review reports and may take action consistent with our Terms of Use and Community Guidelines.

If you are in immediate danger or believe someone faces an emergency, contact local emergency services instead of relying on OnPace support.

## Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your OnPace account from the account or profile settings inside the app.

Account deletion removes your account and associated personal data, except information we are legally required or permitted to retain for purposes such as fraud prevention, accounting, security, dispute resolution, purchase verification, or legal compliance.

If you cannot access the app, email contactonpace@gmail.com from the email address associated with your OnPace account. Use this email subject line:

```text
Account Deletion Request
```

We may request information necessary to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Deleting your OnPace account does not cancel active Apple subscriptions. Cancel subscriptions through Apple Account settings.
