# OnPace Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 4, 2026

OnPace is operated by Tyler King, doing business as OnPace (“OnPace,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

OnPace provides a mobile application that helps people create, organize, and participate in shared goals, including group savings goals, group planning, progress tracking, and related premium features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information OnPace collects, how we use and share it, how long we retain it, and the choices available to you.

By using OnPace, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use OnPace.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information needed to operate OnPace and provide shared-goal features.

### Account Information

When you create or sign in to an OnPace account, we may collect:

- Your name or display name
- Email address
- Authentication identifier
- Profile image, if you choose to provide one
- Account creation, authentication, and sign-in information

### Group and Goal Information

When you use OnPace, we may collect and store information you create, provide, or share through the app, including:

- Goal names, descriptions, categories, target amounts, and target dates
- Group names, group membership, invitations, and invitation status
- Group organizer and member roles
- Savings-progress updates and contribution amounts
- Budget categories, allocations, and planning information
- Privacy settings selected for a goal or group
- Notes, messages, or other content you choose to submit through a group or goal, if those features are available

### Purchase and Premium Information

If you make an in-app purchase, we receive purchase and entitlement information from Apple and RevenueCat, a service used to manage app purchase entitlements.

This information may include:

- OnPace user identifier
- Apple or RevenueCat product identifier
- Purchase and entitlement status
- Subscription renewal, expiration, cancellation, or refund status
- Transaction or purchase identifier

OnPace does not receive or store your full payment-card number. Purchases are processed by Apple through Apple In-App Purchase.

### Device and Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and improve OnPace, including:

- Device model
- Operating-system version
- App version
- Language and regional settings
- IP address or approximate location derived from IP address
- Diagnostic, crash, and performance information
- Technical logs associated with app activity, errors, security events, or feature availability

### Information Related to Invitations

When another user invites you to join a group or goal, we may process information related to that invitation, such as your email address, user identifier, invitation status, and the associated group or goal.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts
- Provide group-goal, shared savings, planning, invitation, and progress-tracking features
- Display information to group members in accordance with group roles and selected privacy settings
- Process, verify, restore, and manage in-app purchase entitlements
- Provide Group Pass and OnPace Plus premium access
- Respond to support requests, feedback, reports, and account inquiries
- Detect, investigate, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms of Use
- Diagnose errors, maintain performance, and improve OnPace
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights

OnPace does not sell your personal information.

## 3. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to provide OnPace, comply with legal obligations, protect users, or operate our business.

### With Other OnPace Users

Information associated with a group or goal may be visible to other members of that group according to the organizer’s selected privacy settings and applicable member permissions.

Depending on a group’s settings, other members may be able to see:

- Your display name
- Your membership in a group
- Goal progress
- Savings amounts or contribution status
- Percentage progress
- Group-level aggregate progress
- Goal-related information you choose to share

Review a group’s privacy settings before joining, contributing to, or sharing information within that group.

### With Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate OnPace. These may include:

- **Supabase**, for authentication, databases, and backend services
- **RevenueCat**, for in-app purchase and subscription entitlement management
- **Apple**, for App Store distribution and in-app purchase processing
- Infrastructure, diagnostics, analytics, or support providers that may be used to operate, secure, and improve OnPace

Service providers may process information only as needed to provide services to us and subject to applicable legal or contractual obligations.

### For Legal, Safety, and Business Reasons

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

- Comply with applicable law, legal process, court order, subpoena, or lawful government request
- Protect the rights, safety, security, and property of OnPace, our users, or others
- Detect or prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, or technical problems
- Enforce our Terms of Use
- Support a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to applicable law

## 4. Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through Apple In-App Purchase.

OnPace may use RevenueCat to help verify and manage purchase entitlements. Apple controls payment processing, billing, subscription management, and refunds according to Apple’s policies.

You may manage or cancel Apple subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide OnPace, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

For example:

- Account, group, goal, and savings-progress information is generally retained while your account remains active.
- Purchase and entitlement records may be retained as necessary for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, and entitlement verification.
- Technical logs and diagnostics may be retained for a limited period necessary for security, troubleshooting, and service improvement.
- When you request account deletion, we delete or de-identify personal information unless we are required or permitted to retain certain information for legal, security, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information. You may also have rights to object to or limit certain processing.

You may:

- Update certain profile information in the app
- Change group or goal privacy settings where you have appropriate permission
- Leave groups where that option is available
- Manage Apple subscriptions through your Apple Account settings
- Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing contactonpace@gmail.com
- Delete your account from within the OnPace app

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

## 7. Account Deletion

You can initiate deletion of your OnPace account from the account or profile settings within the app.

Account deletion removes your OnPace account and associated personal information, except for information we must retain or are permitted to retain for legitimate purposes, including legal compliance, fraud prevention, accounting, security, dispute resolution, and purchase-entitlement verification.

Deleting your OnPace account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. If you have an active auto-renewable subscription, you must cancel it through Apple Account subscription settings to prevent future charges.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. However, no method of transmission, storage, or processing is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and using OnPace responsibly.

## 9. Children

OnPace is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to OnPace without appropriate authorization, contact us at contactonpace@gmail.com.

## 10. International Users

OnPace may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have data-protection laws that differ from the laws of your country.

By using OnPace, you understand that information may be transferred to and processed in locations where we or our service providers operate, subject to applicable law.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the Effective Date above and provide additional notice when required by law.

Your continued use of OnPace after an updated Privacy Policy takes effect means you acknowledge the updated policy to the extent permitted by law.
