# OnPace Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** October 4, 2026

OnPace is designed to help people coordinate shared goals respectfully and safely.

OnPace is operated by Tyler King, doing business as OnPace.

These Community Guidelines apply to all group names, goal names, invitations, profile information, savings updates, notes, messages, and other content or conduct on OnPace.

## Be Respectful

Treat other users with respect.

Do not harass, threaten, bully, shame, discriminate against, stalk, intimidate, or abuse another person.

## Keep Content Appropriate

Do not post, send, or share content that is:

- Hateful, discriminatory, or demeaning
- Sexually explicit or exploitative
- Violent or threatening
- Defamatory or knowingly false
- Designed to embarrass, humiliate, or target another person
- Illegal or intended to facilitate illegal activity

## Do Not Misuse Financial Information

OnPace is a planning and tracking tool. Do not use it to:

- Collect money through deception
- Pressure, threaten, or coerce another user into contributing money
- Offer loans, investments, securities, credit, gambling, or financial services
- Misrepresent a group goal, amount, date, or organizer identity
- Conduct scams, fraud, or impersonation

## Respect Privacy

Do not share another person’s personal, financial, or account information without permission.

Do not attempt to access another person’s account, bypass privacy settings, or misuse information available in a group.

## No Spam or Deceptive Invitations

Do not send repeated unwanted invitations, misleading invitations, spam, or promotional content unrelated to a real shared goal.

## Reporting Violations

If you believe someone has violated these Community Guidelines, report the issue by emailing:

**contactonpace@gmail.com**

Please include:

- The relevant group or goal name
- The display name or account involved, if available
- A description of the issue
- Screenshots, if safe to provide

We may review reported content and take action, including removing content, limiting features, removing users from groups, suspending accounts, or terminating accounts.

If you are in immediate danger or believe someone faces an emergency, contact local emergency services instead of relying on OnPace support.
